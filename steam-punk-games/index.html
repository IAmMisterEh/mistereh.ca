SPIRAL ESCAPE

A steampunk typing challenge!

  • Type letters closest to the ENEMY BUBBLE
  • Each hit pushes enemies back toward center
  • Clear all bubbles before they escape!
  • Build streaks for multiplier bonus

How to Play

Core Mechanics:

  • Enemies spawn at center of spiral
  • Type closest letter to enemy bubble
  • Hits explode bubbles, pushing enemies back
  • Misses lose momentum with visual penalty
  • Win by clearing all enemies or reaching score goal

Progression:

  • Lvl 1-2: Home row (a s d f j k l g h)
  • Lvl 3-4: Top row (e i r t u y q w o p)
  • Lvl 5-6: Bottom row (v b n m z x c , . /)
  • Lvl 7+: Numbers & symbols (! @ # $ % ^ & *)

Visual Elements:

  • Spiral Path: Arched track for enemies to follow
  • Letter Bubbles: Spawn at center, push outward
  • Enemy Sprite: Brass cog following the spiral
  • Particles: Explode on hit for feedback
